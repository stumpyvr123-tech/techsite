Iphone Duo news The iPhone duo officially launched! Here is some info about it: Launch and Sales Status • Sold Out: The iPhone Duo has completely sold out across most major markets following massive initial demand and pre-orders. • Target Met: Apple aimed to ship around 6 million units this year, a target reports indicate has largely been met or accounted for, with no restocks expected during the holiday season. • Resale Prices: Scarcity caused some units to appear on secondary online listings for as much as $5,000. The "Crackgate" Screen Issue • The Problem: Shortly after launch, a number of early buyers reported random inner-screen cracking, bright vertical lines, and a crunching noise near the central hinge within hours or days of use. • Apple's Response: Apple acknowledged that the defect impacts a "small number" of devices and is replacing affected units under AppleCare warranty. Out-of-warranty screen replacements cost $999. • Root Cause Reports: Leakers and industry reports suggest the failure stems from defective materials supplied by one of Apple's two hinge component manufacturers rather than a fundamental flaw in the hinge design itself.
