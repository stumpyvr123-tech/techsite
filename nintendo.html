Here is some new news on the switch and switch 2

Nintendo has released System Update 23.5.0 for both the Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, patching out recent jailbreak exploits and adding preparation for a new family group eShop gifting feature.

Key Nintendo Updates (November 2026)

• System Update 23.5.0: Live now for Switch and Switch 2, this update closes the OS exploit used in recent homebrew/jailbreaking attempts.

eShop Digital Gifting: Prepares support to let family group members gift digital games, DLC, and upgrade packs, officially rolling out on November 17, 2026 (with some regions referencing Nov. 16).

• Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake: President Shuntaro Furukawa confirmed the new remake is off to a "great start," while dataminers have spotted hints of a potential Master Quest mode in the game files.

• New LEGO Yoshi Teased: Nintendo teased an upcoming adult-targeted 18+ LEGO Super Mario Yoshi set on the Nintendo Today app, slated for a 2027 release.

• Switch 2 Price Stability: Despite a 37% year-on-year dip in hardware sales and a 16% drop in profits, Nintendo announced it has enough stock to maintain the current Switch 2 pricing without any planned price hikes.

• Call of Duty: Warzone: Officially launching on the Nintendo Switch 2 as part of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Season 1.
