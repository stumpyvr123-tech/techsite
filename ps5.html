Here are some new things on Ps5.

Rockstar Games officially announced that Grand Theft Auto 6 will target 30 frames per second (FPS) across all platforms, including the PS5 Pro. However, the PS5 Pro will feature exclusive visual upgrades, including AI-driven upscaling via PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) and exclusive ray-traced shadows. The game also supports DualSense haptic feedback and Tempest 3D audio on PS5.

GTA 6 Preloads for GTA 6 have officially begun ahead of its worldwide release on November 19, 2026.

Silent Hill 2 2024 86% · PlayStation 5 +2 Bloober Team released a surprise patch (1.008) for Silent Hill 2 on PS5 that finally resolves graphical, lighting, shadow, and flickering bugs specifically affecting the PS5 Pro.

New PS Portal Hardware Refresh Sony revealed a new model of the PlayStation Portal launching on December 10, 2026, which adds internal storage (addressing a major limitation of the original) and includes a month of PS Plus Premium.

Fairgames Cancelled Sony and Haven Studio officially cancelled the multiplayer live-service shooter Fairgames after several years in development, though it resulted in no studio layoffs.
